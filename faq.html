Frequently asked questions

Quick, simple answers about REYVO.

What is REYVO?

REYVO is a functional performance drink designed to support steady energy, hydration and focus in your daily routine.

What is the difference between Zestiva and Velora?

Zestiva is our citrus-forward energy and focus formula. Velora has a berry profile and is created for hydration and performance support.

Does REYVO contain added sugar?

No. Our drinks are made without added sugar. Please check the pack for the latest nutrition information.

Is REYVO vegan-friendly?

Yes, our current formulas are vegan-friendly.

When should I drink REYVO?

Enjoy it before work, studying, training, travel or whenever your day needs a functional lift.

Can I drink REYVO every day?

REYVO is designed for daily routines. Follow the serving guidance on the pack and speak with a healthcare professional if you are sensitive to caffeine or unsure.

How much caffeine does it contain?

Caffeine content varies by product. Check the relevant product page and pack label for the exact amount.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across serviceable locations in India. Your availability and delivery estimate are confirmed at checkout.

How can I track my order?

Tracking details are sent to your email or phone number once your order has shipped.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Contact us with your order number and clear photos of the package and damaged items, and our support team will help.
