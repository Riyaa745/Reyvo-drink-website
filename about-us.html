OUR STORY

BUILT FROM A REAL PROBLEM.

Not a trend. Not a boardroom idea. REYVO was built from 18 years of lived experience inside high-pressure environments - where the usual solutions kept failing.

Pressure

Long days made energy, focus, and recovery feel like separate battles.

Pattern

Coffee, chai, energy drinks helped briefly, then created another dip.

Shift

REYVO was built for daily rhythm: clean output by day, calmer recovery by night.

THE PATTERN

The biggest enemy of ambition is the body not keeping up with the mind.

Long days. Constant decisions. Coffee to start. Another cup to keep going. Sometimes an energy drink when things really slow down.

It works for a while. Then it doesn't. Energy spikes, then drops. Focus becomes inconsistent. And the cycle repeats - for years, sometimes decades.

What's missing isn't willpower. It's not more caffeine. It's a more balanced approach - something designed to work with how the body actually functions, not against it.

That gap is where REYVO began.

Vishwesh W.

Founder & CEO

"REYVO wasn't created to sell a new habit. It was created to improve an existing one."

I have spent 18 years in rooms where the pressure never let up, a Hilton Hotel in London, a Starbucks counter in Mumbai, then a decade climbing from Station Manager to General Manager across Amazon and Myntra, running operations that moved 2,00,000+ shipments a day. Coffee got me through all of it. By the third cup on a bad day, I'd be sitting in the meeting that mattered most with my hands slightly shaking and my focus already fraying. I told myself that was just the cost of the job. It wasn't. I just hadn't found a better tool.

When I co-founded SIPL in 2022, the stakes went up again. Building a logistics company and scaling it across 9 cities meant there was no version of "off" anymore. Every bad decision was mine alone. That's when the coffee cycle stopped being a minor annoyance and started being a real liability. I needed my body to keep up with decisions I couldn't afford to get wrong. Nothing on the shelf was built for that. So I started building it myself.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Philosophy before product

These aren't values written after building the product. They're the beliefs that made us build it at all.

Ambition & health don't compete

You shouldn't have to choose between performing at your best and treating your body well. The two are not in conflict - they've just been offered conflicting tools until now.

Consistency over spikes

The biggest challenge for ambitious people isn't intent - it's consistency. A steady, sustained experience is worth more than dramatic short-term bursts followed by crashes.

Clean by conviction, clear by default

We use ingredients with intention, not because "clean" is fashionable - every ingredient has a defined role, nothing added for marketing. And we speak plainly about what's in our products and why. No vague claims. No borrowed science.

Community before commerce

We build relationships before revenue. Trust is earned first - sales follow. The REYVO community matters more to us than any single transaction.

Founder honesty

REYVO is built from lived experience. We share that journey openly - the decisions, the trade-offs, and the learnings. Transparency is a strength, not a risk.

OUR VALUES

Not aspirational statements. Operational commitments.

01

Performance without compromise

We don't trade function for aesthetics, or short-term results for long-term balance. Everything is designed to support performance in a way that works with the body, not against it.
