Frequently Asked

Will this make me sleepy?

No. Velora is designed for a calm, clear-headed wind-down - not sedation. You should feel relaxed and settled, not drowsy.

Is Velora a sleep aid?

No. Velora supports a calm evening transition. It is not formulated, tested, or intended to treat sleep difficulties, and is not a substitute for medical care if you have ongoing sleep issues.

Can I take it daily?

Yes. Velora is formulated for regular daily use as part of an evening routine, with 30mg of natural caffeine - well within safe daily limits for adults.

When should I take it?

Best in the late afternoon to early evening, as your workday winds down. Not intended as a substitute for consistent sleep habits.
