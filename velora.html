Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Will this make me sleepy?

No. Velora is designed to support relaxation, not force sleep. The L-Theanine promotes calm alertness - you'll feel settled, not sedated.

Can I take it daily?

Yes, designed for regular use as part of your evening routine. Most users find it most effective as a consistent wind-down habit rather than occasional use.

Does it replace sleep aids?

Velora makes no sleep claims. It can serve as a more natural part of your wind-down routine - addressing mental noise before sleep, not replacing prescribed medication.

When should I take it?

In the evening or when transitioning out of your workday. Ideally 1-2 hours before you plan to rest. Works especially well on high-stress days.
