Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Is it safe for daily use?

Yes. Zestiva is formulated specifically for regular daily consumption. The caffeine level of 70mg per serving is well within safe daily limits for adults.

Does it replace coffee?

It can be used as a full alternative depending on your routine. Many people use Zestiva for their afternoon slot instead of a second or third coffee.

Will it cause jitters?

Zestiva is formulated for a more balanced experience. The L-Theanine specifically moderates the caffeine response, reducing the likelihood of jitters compared to conventional stimulants.

When is the best time?

Anytime you need support for alertness during the day. Morning to start, or mid-day to avoid the afternoon dip. Not recommended within 4-5 hours of intended sleep time.

Is it suitable for everyone?

Not recommended for children under 12, pregnant women, lactating mothers, or caffeine-sensitive individuals. Consult your doctor if you have a medical condition.
